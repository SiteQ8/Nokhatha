Nokhatha reminds you to look after your home and your car and keeps an eye on your subscriptions, with every date set to the Gulf's traditional year from Suhail to Al-Kulaibain instead of a generic calendar that knows nothing about our summer or the Bawarih winds.

AC filters come every month and every two weeks during the Bawarih, AC servicing lands before Al-Kinna, the roof and drains get checked before Al-Wasm, the water heater before Al-Murabbaniya, and tires, battery and the car's AC before the summer.

• Home: ACs, water tank and filter, heater, roof, safety and pest control, with a list for every house, apartment, chalet, farm or jakhoor
• Car: oil and filters by kilometres or time, whichever comes first, an odometer estimated from your readings, and registration, insurance and inspection before they expire
• Subscriptions: what you pay each month and year, and before every renewal a question about whether you still use it, so you cancel what you don't need before the money leaves
• My belongings: a boat, motorbike, generator, pool, garden, camp or pet, or anything you name yourself with your own tasks
• The year dial: where we are in the seasons and what is coming, at a glance

Your privacy is kept by construction: the app has no internet permission at all, so there is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your phone and reminders are worked out on the phone itself.

Works in Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman with your currency, and the code is open.
